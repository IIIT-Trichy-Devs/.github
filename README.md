# IIIT Trichy Devs

### Learn. Build. Contribute. Together.

Welcome to **IIIT Trichy Devs** — a student-led developer community at the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Tiruchirappalli.

A place for students who love technology, want to build meaningful projects, explore open source, share knowledge, and grow together.

## What we're about

- **Build together:** Collaborate on projects and turn ideas into reality.
- **Open source:** Learn by contributing to real-world projects.
- **Learn together:** Share resources, experiences, and technical knowledge.
- **Grow together:** Participate in hackathons, workshops, and community initiatives.

## Get involved

Whether you're just starting your coding journey or already building projects, there's a place for you here.

Explore our repositories, contribute to projects, share ideas, and connect with fellow developers.
